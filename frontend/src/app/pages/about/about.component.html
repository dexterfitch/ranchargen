About

Ever needed a spark of inspiration for your next character? Whether you're a doodler, a worldbuilder, or just really into weird goblins with glowing orbs, this app has your back.

Click once to generate a totally random character. Click again to change everything. Or head to the Advanced Edit mode to lock in your favorite traits and mix up the rest.

Built With

  • Angular (Frontend)
  • Flask (Backend API)
  • SQLite (Database)
  • Bootstrap (Layout)

Designed for fun. Created for artists, writers, roleplayers, and anyone who needs a nudge in their creative process.
